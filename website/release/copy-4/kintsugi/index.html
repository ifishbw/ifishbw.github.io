Timeline LA

Timeline LA is a top-down shooter that answers the question: what if Hotline Miami had multiversal time travel? This project was originally made for a 48-hour game jam. After the jam, my two other team members and I kept working on it for a couple of months.

Engine
Godot
Role
Level Designer /
Artist / Sound
Team Size
3 members
Status
Playable Demo
development paused

Contribution

  • Created the art style and all art assets.
  • Created all sound effects and sourced the music.
  • Programmed the bullet-time mechanic.
  • Designed 7 of the 10 levels.

Outcome

  • Learned how to use Godot competently.
  • Best sound design for any project I worked on.
  • A lot of level design lessons learned from making some poorly designed levels.
  • Fantastic being able to create and experiment with a unique art style.
